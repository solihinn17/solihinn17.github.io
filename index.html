About Me

Solihin is a learner. Yes, he views himself as only and always a learner. Armed with nothing but pure boldness, his academic journey began with a blind leap that eventually led him to get lost. Fortunately, he ended up being lost in the right direction (maybe). Currently pursuing his Master's degree at Department of Physics, Universitas Indonesia, his research focuses on theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, where he actively explores the fascinating properties of magnonic materials, the physics of cavity magnonics, and the microscopic mechanisms of superconductivity.
